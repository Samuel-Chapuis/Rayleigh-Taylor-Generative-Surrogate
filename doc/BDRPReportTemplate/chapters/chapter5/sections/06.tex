\section{Wavelet-based results and comparison with baseline diffusion}

At this stage, the evaluation of the wavelet-based diffusion model remains
primarily qualitative. The generated coefficient fields shown in
Figures~\ref{fig:ch5_wavelet_coefficients} and
\ref{fig:ch5_wavelet_reconstruction} demonstrate that the conditional
diffusion framework is capable of learning non-trivial relationships between
the coarse approximation and the associated detail coefficients. The wavelet
representation also makes it easier to localize the origin of reconstruction
errors because the approximation and detail channels are analyzed separately.

\begin{figure}[H]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.95\textwidth]{figures/ch5/wavelet_cascade_coefficients_comparison.png}
    \caption{Comparison of final wavelet coefficient maps at level $j=1$ for the cascade model. For each example, the reference coefficients and the cascade-generated coefficients are shown for the approximation channel $c_A^1$ and the three detail channels $c_H^1$, $c_V^1$, and $c_D^1$.}
    \label{fig:ch5_wavelet_coefficients}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[H]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.82\textwidth]{figures/ch5/wavelet_cascade_reconstruction_comparison.png}
    \caption{RTI fields reconstructed from the final cascade wavelet coefficients. For each example, the reference field, the cascade-generated field, and the absolute pointwise difference are shown.}
    \label{fig:ch5_wavelet_reconstruction}
\end{figure}

The reconstructed density fields nevertheless reveal several limitations. While
the large-scale organization imposed by the approximation coefficients is
generally preserved, the synthesized detail coefficients often introduce
artifacts and yield reconstructions that remain visibly rougher than the
reference DNS fields. In qualitative comparison, the direct image-space
baseline currently produces smoother samples that are visually closer to the
target distribution.

This comparison must, however, be interpreted with care. Unlike the baseline,
the wavelet formulation has not yet been evaluated with the full set of
quantitative metrics introduced in this chapter. At present, the evidence is
therefore sufficient to conclude that the wavelet pipeline captures meaningful
coarse-to-fine dependencies, but not yet sufficient to claim competitive
generative quality with respect to the baseline SGM.
